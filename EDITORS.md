# Updating news & events — the easy guide

You can keep the website's **news** and **events** current without touching any
code. News comes from a Google Sheet; events come from Google Calendar. Change
those, and the website updates itself within about an hour.

Both live in the **group's Google account** — sign in there to edit them. (If you
don't have the login, ask whoever organizes the group.)

---

## Events — use Google Calendar

1. Open the group's Google Calendar.
2. Add, edit, or delete an event the normal way.
3. Done. It appears on the website (home page "Upcoming events" list, and the
   full Events page) on its own.

That's it — no other step.

---

## News — add a row to the Google Sheet

1. Open the group's **News** Google Sheet (bookmark it).
2. Go to the first empty row.
3. Fill in the columns:
   - **title** — the headline (required)
   - **body** — a sentence or two (optional)
   - **date** — like `2026-11-18` (optional, but keeps items in order)
   - **link** — a web address for "read more" (optional)
4. Done. It appears in "Latest news" on the home page within about an hour.

To remove a news item, delete its row. **Don't rename or reorder the top row**
(the column headers) — those have to stay as they are.

---

## "Last updated" note — what it means

Under each section the site quietly shows when it last refreshed
("Updated 2 days ago"). If a section can't reach Google for a while, it keeps
showing the last news/events it had, and that note turns amber and says it may
be out of date. That's your cue that something needs a look — usually just that
a link or sharing setting changed. The site never shows a broken error to
visitors; it just keeps showing the last good information.

## If news or events ever look stuck
- Check the Sheet is still **published to the web** (File → Share → Publish to web).
- Check the Calendar is still **public**.
- Wait up to an hour (the site refreshes on a timer, not instantly).
- Still stuck? Contact whoever maintains the site files.
